What Remark does

Remark lets you write and draw on a photo or a screenshot, then save or share a clean copy: a name or a note on a receipt, an arrow to a button in a screenshot, numbered points on a photo.

The marks become part of a brand-new picture. Remark draws them into new pixels and saves a new file made only from what you see: there are no layers that someone could peel off, and none of the hidden details about you or your phone (such as where a photo was taken) come along.

Marking up a picture

  1. Tap Open photos (your gallery) or Browse files (any folder: Downloads, a USB stick, a cloud drive on your phone). Several pictures at once is fine, up to 20. On a computer you can also drop pictures onto the page, or paste a screenshot with Ctrl+V. In the Android app you can also share pictures to Remark from your gallery or another app.
  2. One picture opens straight away; with several, tap the one to mark up.
  3. Text is ready: tap where the words go, type, and tap Done (or press Enter).
  4. Add anything else with the other tools (see The tools), then tap Done at the top. To place things exactly, pinch to zoom in (scroll on a computer) and move the picture with two fingers.
  5. Remark makes the new picture, checks it (see Saving) and shows it. Tap Save or Share, or Copy picture on a computer to paste it straight into a chat or an email.

The tools

  • Text: tap where the words go and type. The words appear on the picture as you type. Shift+Enter starts a new line. The last few things you typed are offered above the text box, so a name you write on every receipt is one tap.
  • Callout: a text label with a pointer, like a speech bubble. Tap where the words go and type, then drag the round handle at the pointer's tip to what it's about.
  • Arrow: drag from where the arrow starts to what it points at.
  • Line: a straight line, for underlining or crossing out.
  • Circle: drag across what you want to circle. It is an oval, so it fits around anything.
  • Box: drag across what you want to box.
  • Number: tap to place 1, 2, 3… in filled circles. Delete one and the others count again from 1.
  • Highlight: drag over text like a highlighter pen. The text underneath stays readable.
  • Pen: draw freehand: a tick, an underline, a signature.
  • Cover: drag across something to hide it (see below).
  • Crop: drag the corners to keep only part of the picture. Whole picture undoes the crop; Crop done goes back to Text.

Text that reads well anywhere

Text and callouts come in three styles:

  • Label (the usual one): the words on a solid rounded box in your colour, with black or white letters, whichever reads better. Readable on a busy photo, a white receipt or a dark screen.
  • Outline: coloured letters with a contrasting edge.
  • Plain: just the coloured letters.

Text is sized for the picture: the first tap gives readable text on a large photo and on a phone screenshot alike. S, M and L make it smaller or larger. English and Thai text use the app's own typefaces, so they look the same everywhere; other writing uses your device's fonts.

Colours and sizes

Six colours (red, yellow, green, blue, black and white) and three sizes (S, M, L) apply to the next mark, or to the selected one. Lines, arrows and shapes get a faint edge in black or white, so red still shows on a red background. The highlighter has its own colour (yellow at first).

Changing a mark

  • Tap a mark to select it (a dashed outline appears). Drag it to move it.
  • Drag its round handles to change it: both ends of an arrow or a line, the tip of a callout's pointer, the corners of a circle, a box or a cover.
  • Change its colour, size or style with the buttons under the tools.
  • Edit text (or tap a selected text again) changes its words; Delete removes the mark.
  • Tap empty space to deselect.
  • Undo and Redo (the arrows at the top) take back any change.
  • Pinch to zoom (or scroll with the mouse wheel), and move the picture with two fingers.
  • ‹ › go to the previous and next picture.

On a computer, Delete removes the selected mark, and Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y undo and redo.

Covers

A cover hides a stray detail: an account number on a statement, a name in a screenshot, a face in the background. It can be Black, White, or Colour from around: the colour of what surrounds it (the paper, the screen's background), so a cover on a receipt barely shows.

Only a cover hides anything. A highlighter, a box, a line or a label over something still shows it, or lets it show through. A cover replaces the pixels underneath, and it is always drawn on top of every other mark.

To find faces, number plates, card numbers and more automatically, use the studio's Redact app.

Saving

When you tap Done, Remark:

  1. opens the original again at full size and keeps the cropped part, if you cropped;
  2. draws your marks into it, then paints the covers, and saves a new file from those pixels alone;
  3. opens the new file again and checks, pixel by pixel, that every cover came out (if one didn't, the picture can't be saved);
  4. checks that the new file carries no hidden data, and lists what the original carried (where the photo was taken, the phone, the date and time).

Then look at the picture, and tap Save, Share or Copy picture. Screenshots and other PNG pictures are saved as PNG (sharp text), photos as JPEG; Settings can change this. The new file gets a plain name such as remark-1.jpg: a file's name goes with it, and camera names say when a photo was taken. Settings can keep the original name instead (with -remark added). In the Android app, the save screen opens in the original's folder.

Copy picture (computers) puts the picture on your computer's clipboard. If your system syncs the clipboard to other devices (Windows clipboard sync, Apple's Universal Clipboard), it goes there too; use Save if that matters.

Your original stays as it was: it is still in your gallery, and in any cloud backup.

Settings

  • Save as: the same kind as the original, PNG or JPEG.
  • Names of saved pictures: plain (remark-1.jpg, the default) or the original name with -remark added.
  • Remember these settings on this device: the format, the names, and your last colour and text style. Without it, settings last until you close Remark.
  • Also remember recent texts: keeps the last few things you typed on this device. Without it, they are forgotten when you close Remark. Forget recent texts clears them.

Privacy

  • Nothing leaves your device. The page's security policy blocks every connection, so it can't send anything anywhere, and the Android app has no internet permission.
  • Opening the web app is an ordinary website visit: the server sees your connection, as for any website. The pictures you open are never sent.
  • Everything Remark needs, its typefaces included, is built into the page: nothing is downloaded while you use it.
  • Pictures exist only while Remark is open. The only things kept on your device are your settings and recent texts, if you ask, and a count of pictures saved (for an occasional donation request).
  • Pictures, the text you type and file names are kept from your browser's translator, so translation never sends them anywhere.
