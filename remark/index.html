Remark

Add a name or a note to a receipt or a screenshot in seconds, with text that stays readable on any background. Point things out with callouts, arrows, circles, boxes and numbered markers, highlight or draw freehand, and cover a stray detail. Remark saves a fresh copy with the location and phone details removed.

Free. Works in any modern browser on a phone, tablet or computer. No account, no upload: your pictures never leave your device.

NewAlso as an Android app: share pictures to Remark from your gallery or a chat, and it has no internet access at all.

What it does

Text that reads anywhere

Words sit on a label in your colour, with black or white letters, whichever reads better: on a busy photo, a white receipt or a dark screen. English and Thai text look the same on every device.

Point things out

Callouts, arrows, lines, circles, boxes and numbered markers 1, 2, 3. Delete one and the others count again.

Highlight and draw

A highlighter that keeps the text underneath readable, and a pen for a tick, an underline or a signature.

Cover a stray detail

Black, white or the colour around it, so a cover on a receipt barely shows. A cover replaces the pixels underneath and is drawn on top of everything. For faces and card numbers found for you, use Redact.

Quick for repeat notes

The last few things you typed are offered above the text box, so a name you write on every receipt is one tap. Crop to keep only what matters.

A clean copy

Your marks are drawn into a brand-new picture, checked pixel by pixel, and cleaned of where the photo was taken and which phone took it. No layers anyone could peel off.

Many pictures at once

Up to 20 photos or screenshots. In the Android app, share them to Remark from your gallery or a chat; on a computer, paste a screenshot with Ctrl+V.

Private by design

No account, no server, no analytics. The web app blocks every network connection and the Android app has no internet permission.

What you need

  • Any modern browser on a phone, tablet or computer: Chrome, Edge, Brave, Firefox or Safari.
  • Or the Android app, which adds Share → Remark.
  • Pictures in JPEG, PNG or WebP. HEIC photos need converting to JPEG first.

Questions

Is Remark really free?

Yes. No subscription, no account and no ads. If you find it useful, you can support it on Ko-fi.

Are my pictures uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything happens on your own device. The web app blocks every network connection, and the Android app has no internet permission.

Does a highlight or a box hide what’s under it?

No: only a cover hides anything. A highlighter, a box, a line or a label still lets things show through. A cover replaces the pixels underneath.

I opened Remark from a link in LINE and can’t save

LINE’s built-in browser can’t save or share files. Remark shows a banner with Open in your browser: tap it, and everything works.

Why isn’t the Android app on Google Play?

Publishing there means registering with Google and handing over government ID, and Google is extending that check to apps installed from elsewhere. We think you should be able to install software you choose on a phone you own, so we support Keep Android Open and offer the app directly (more on why). The Android page explains how to install it and check it’s the official build.

Is the code open?

Remark is free software under the GNU GPL v3. The source code is on GitHub, and every Android release is built from it automatically, with a checksum to verify the download.

Remark is provided as it is, without any warranty. Check every picture before you share it.
