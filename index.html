Small apps that each do one job well. Free to use, open source, and built so your data never leaves your device.

  1. Released October 2026

    Unmarked

    Photos and documents carry details you can’t see: where a photo was taken, which phone took it, who wrote a document and what was deleted from it. Unmarked shows you what’s there and makes a clean copy without it, for photos and PDFs, many at once.

    Works in any modern browser on a phone, tablet or computer. Photos are cleaned without any loss of quality, and your files never leave your device.

    NewThe Android app: share photos and PDFs to Unmarked straight from your gallery, and it has no internet access at all.

  2. Released October 2026

    Rounds

    A loud, clear interval timer. Pick from 16 presets, from Tabata and EMOM to boxing rounds, sorted by goal or hardest first, or build your own. Three beeps count you into and out of every work round, with full-screen colour so you can read it from across the room.

    Works in any modern browser, offline once added to your home screen. Optional live heart rate from any standard Bluetooth strap or watch, with Chrome or Chromium on Android or Bluefy on iPhone; readings are shown only, never saved or sent.

    NewHeart-rate zones: set your max heart rate (or your age), connect a strap, and a ring around the timer shows your zone as you work, with your time in each zone at the end.

  3. Released October 2026

    Still Point

    A calm morning check-in with a Polar H10: a two-minute heart rate variability reading compared with your own usual range, an optional standing test, and a soothing breathing guide from 3 to 7 breaths a minute that finds your resonance pace.

    Needs a Polar H10 or any standard Bluetooth chest strap, and Google Chrome or Chromium on Android or the Android app; a simulated strap lets you try it without one. For wellness only; not a medical device.

    NewThe Android app: readings and breathing sessions keep going with the screen off, and it has no internet access at all.

  4. Released October 2026

    Lead One ECG

    Record the ECG from a Polar H10 chest strap and get a clear, multi-page report you can save as a PDF: rhythm, heart rate variability, breathing, and recovery after training.

    Needs a Polar H10. Runs in Chrome or Chromium on Android, Chrome or Edge on a computer, or Bluefy on iPhone and iPad. For wellness and education only; not a medical device.

    NewThe Android app: keeps recording with the screen off, and has no internet access at all.
